How do releases work?

Retrieved from work-drive · 7/28/2026, 2:42:00 PM

Our releases follow trunk-based development with short-lived feature branches and automated delivery from main.

Plan against the roadmap, build behind feature flags, validate the pull request, then cut and monitor the release. Roll back to the previous stable tag if health checks regress.
